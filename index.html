Everything you need to know about Taggy smart cards and enterprise rollouts.

Creating your Taggy card takes less than two minutes. Choose your template, enter your contact information, upload your logo, and your digital business card is instantly live and ready to share via QR, Apple Wallet, or link.

You can sign up for a team account in the dashboard or book a demo with our enterprise specialists. We provide centralized billing, employee directory sync (SCIM), template locking, and custom embossed batch card production.

Yes! Our individual digital card is completely free forever. You can create your card, share via QR code or link, and receive contacts without paying a cent. Physical embossed cards and enterprise team features are available as upgrades.

Physical custom embossed Taggy cards are a one-time purchase with no mandatory monthly subscription. Premium matte cards start at $29 with free worldwide shipping.

Recipients do not need the Taggy app or any software installed! When you tap your card to their smartphone or show your QR code, your interactive profile opens immediately in their default browser, and they can save your vCard directly into their phone contacts.

Taggy cards contain an embedded contactless smart NFC chip and high-density antenna. When brought within 2-4 centimeters of a smartphone, the phone wirelessly reads your encrypted link and opens your card instantly.
